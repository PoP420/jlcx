# Loan Amortization Calculator Specifications

## 1. Given Data & Variables

The calculator must use the following base variables and default values to perform calculations:

| Variable | Description | Default Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **$P$** | **Loan Amount (Principal)** | `100,000` |
| **$n$** | **Term (in months)** | `9` |
| **$r$** | **Monthly Interest Rate** | `5%` (`0.05`) |
| **$p$** | **Penalty Rate** | `6%` (`0.06`) |

---

## 2. Mathematical Formulas

### Method A: Diminishing Balance (Equal Principal)
*In this method, the principal payment remains constant, while the interest decreases as the remaining balance goes down.*

**1. Monthly Principal Payment ($M_p$)**
$$M_p = \frac{P}{n}$$

**2. Monthly Interest for Month $i$ ($I_i$)**
$$I_i = B_{i-1} \times r$$
*(Where $B_{i-1}$ is the Remaining Balance from the previous month. For Month 1, $B_0 = P$)*

**3. Monthly Total Payable for Month $i$ ($T_i$)**
$$T_i = M_p + I_i$$

**4. Remaining Balance after Month $i$ ($B_i$)**
$$B_i = B_{i-1} - M_p$$

---

### Method B: Flat Rate
*In this method, interest is calculated on the original loan amount for the entire term. Both principal and interest remain constant every month.*

**1. Monthly Principal Payment ($M_p$)**
$$M_p = \frac{P}{n}$$

**2. Monthly Interest ($I$)**
$$I = P \times r$$
*(This value is constant for all months)*

**3. Monthly Total Payable ($T$)**
$$T = M_p + I$$
*(This value is constant for all months)*

---

### Penalty Calculation (Applies to both methods)
*If a scheduled payment is overdue, a penalty is applied to that specific month's total payable.*

**1. Penalty Amount ($Pen_i$)**
$$Pen_i = T_i \times p$$

**2. Total Due with Penalty ($Total_i$)**
$$Total_i = T_i + Pen_i$$

---

## 3. Summary & Totals Formulas

At the end of the term ($n$), the following totals must be calculated for the schedule summary:

**1. Total Principal Paid**
$$\text{Total Principal} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} M_p$$

**2. Total Interest Paid**
$$\text{Total Interest} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} I_i$$

**3. Total Penalties**
$$\text{Total Penalties} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} Pen_i$$

**4. Grand Total Payable**
$$\text{Grand Total} = \text{Total Principal} + \text{Total Interest} + \text{Total Penalties}$$
